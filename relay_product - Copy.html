Category: Agency SaaS Primary user: Account manager Channels: WhatsApp · Email · Calls MVP: 6 clients · 1 AM · 13 steps
01 — Name
Why Relay
The word earns its keep on multiple registers. In sport, a relay is seamless handoff — one runner to the next, no dropped baton. In communications, a relay is the station that receives and forwards a signal faithfully. In both cases, the message gets through. That is exactly what the product does: it receives what clients send, reasons about it, and relays the right response at the right moment — without the AM having to be the one holding the baton at every step.

02 — Positioning
What Relay is — precisely
The positioning must be specific enough to own, broad enough to sell. Three framings, each for a different conversation.
One-liner (for introductions)
Relay is the account manager that never sleeps. It handles every client touchpoint from brief to delivery — automatically — and brings your AM in only when a human decision is actually needed.
Category (for analyst conversations)
Agentic client servicing software for creative agencies. A workflow engine with AI agents that manage the full project lifecycle — intake, pricing, onboarding, production, feedback, and retention — across WhatsApp and email.
For agencies (the sales pitch)
Your AM should spend their time on relationships, not admin. Relay handles every message, every follow-up, every status update, and every change request — and brings your AM in only when a real decision is required. One AM can manage 4× the clients.
What it is not
Not a CRM. Not a project management tool. Not a chatbot. Not an email client. Relay is an agent-native operating layer that sits above your communication channels and below your creative team — the system that runs the relationship.
03 — Feature set
The 13-step workflow engine
Every project runs through one workflow. 13 steps, 5 phases. Each step is either auto-executed by an agent, or queued for the AM to review. The AM is a dashboard reviewer — not an executor.
1
Conversion
Brief intake & structuring
Reads inbound WhatsApp or email. Classifies project type, scores completeness, extracts all fields, surfaces missing information as structured gap questions.
Auto
Evaluation & pricing
Scores project fit against your capabilities. Computes a line-item quote from the pricing matrix. Queues for AM to approve, override, or reject before anything goes to the client.
AM gate
Service agreement
Generates PAYG or retainer agreement from template, variables filled from the approved project scope. Sent via email (always — formal document).
Auto
2
Onboarding
Asset collection
Generates a checklist of required inputs per project type. Sends via WhatsApp. Tracks what's received. Escalates overdue items automatically. Production cannot start until complete.
Auto
3
Production
Milestone communication
Proactively tells the client where the project stands before they ask. Triggered by production stage changes. Eliminates "just checking in" messages.
Auto
Approval & sign-off
Sends a formal sign-off request at each production gate (concept, script, rough cut). Locks the approved stage. Reminder sequence if no response. Sign-off via WhatsApp prompt + email record.
Auto
4
Feedback & revisions
Tactical feedback processing
Reads every inbound feedback message. Classifies: in-scope or out-of-scope, workflow-implementable or human-editor needed. Drafts a response for AM review. Out-of-scope feedback generates a change order proposal.
AM review
Revision round tracking
Tracks rounds used vs contracted. Warns the client at 1 round remaining. Blocks further revisions without a change order once exhausted.
Auto
Strategic feedback triage
Identifies upstream, out-of-scope feedback from high-value clients. Researches the topic, prepares a summary report for the AM. AM decides whether to engage.
AM gate
5
Retention & growth
Escalation triage
Monitors sentiment across every message. Detects frustration early and flags it before the client escalates. When escalation happens, classifies reason and prepares AM context. AM decides: resolve async, schedule call, or decline.
AM gate
Upsell identification
At natural moments (delivery, positive feedback, retainer renewal), surfaces upsell opportunities based on client type and seasonal signals. AM decides whether and when to pitch.
AM gate
Relationship health scoring
Continuously scores every client account across sentiment, engagement, revision volume, and response time. Green / amber / red bands. AM sees health on the dashboard. Red triggers an automatic flag.
Background
Conversation intelligence
Indexes every message, email, and call transcript. Extracts commitments, decisions, and signals. Feeds health scoring and provides agents with long-term memory of the client relationship.
Background
04 — Communication layer
One thread. Every channel.
Every message from every channel — WhatsApp, email, call recordings — is normalised into a single chronological thread per project. The AM reads one stream, not three inboxes. Every outbound message is sent from the dashboard. Every inbound message is automatically attributed to the right sender from the people roster.
WhatsApp groups
One group per client. All messages read and sent via WhatsApp MCP. Teams sending directly are also captured and attributed.
MVP
Email (Outlook)
Shared team inbox polled every 5 minutes. Inbound emails matched to client by sender address. Outbound from AM's account.
MVP
Call recordings
AM uploads mp3. Gemini transcribes with tone preservation. Summary and full transcript written into the project thread.
MVP
People roster
Every contact tagged Client or BlinkofAI. Automatic attribution on every message. Unrecognised senders flagged for AM to add.
MVP
05 — Additional features
What else belongs in Relay
Derived from first principles, the workflow definition, and the data model. These are not nice-to-haves — they are the features that complete the product.
Communication obligation tracker
Every step has an expected outbound message. The system tracks whether it was sent, skipped, or is overdue. Nothing falls through the gap.
MVP
AM decision queue
Every item requiring AM judgment — evaluation approval, strategic feedback, escalation — queued with full agent reasoning and a one-click action. AM spends 30 seconds per decision, not 30 minutes.
MVP
Skip with consequence
AM can skip any communication obligation. Gate-step skips require AM to confirm they accept the consequence. Every skip is logged with reason.
MVP
Sign-off locking
Every approved stage is locked. A client who "changes their mind" on an approved concept raises a change request — not a revision. The system enforces this contractually.
MVP
Change order engine
Out-of-scope feedback automatically generates a priced change order. AM approves, client agrees. No informal scope creep.
MVP
Brief versioning
The original brief is sacred — never edited. Structured versions are tracked as brief_v1, v2, brief_final. Full audit trail of what the client asked for.
MVP
Call transcript library
Every call — transcribed, summarised, tagged (key decisions, upsell signals, commitments). Searchable. Linked to the project thread at the exact chronological position.
MVP
Workflow template editor
Agencies edit their own workflow — add steps, change delivery modes, set channel defaults, configure triggers. No code deploy needed.
v2
Portfolio analytics
Cross-project insights: average health score, revision rate by client type, escalation frequency, AM response time, revenue pipeline. The agency learns over time.
v2
Connector marketplace
Agencies enable connectors from a library: Slack, Notion, HubSpot, Razorpay, Google Drive. Each is an MCP server. Enable = one click. No code changes.
v2
Multi-AM support
Multiple AMs per agency, each owning specific clients. AM queue scoped per AM. Project assignment and notification routing.
v2
Pattern recognition
Cross-project intelligence: which brief types have the most revision rounds, which client segments escalate most, where scope creep originates. The system gets smarter as it processes more projects.
v3
Client portal
Optional login for clients to upload assets, check project status, and submit formal approvals. Reduces WhatsApp dependency for structured actions.
v3
Agency benchmarks
Anonymous benchmarks across Relay agencies: median project duration, average revision rounds, escalation rates by industry. Agencies see where they stand.
v3
06 — Technical foundation
Built to grow into a platform
MVP uses the simplest viable stack. Architecture is designed so the SaaS layer (multi-tenancy, connector marketplace, analytics) can be added on top without rebuilding the core.
Frontend
React + TypeScript
TanStack Query for server state. Firestore real-time listeners for live updates. One dashboard — AM only for MVP.
API layer
FastAPI (Python)
REST endpoints for AM actions. Firebase Admin SDK for token verification. Inbound webhook receivers for WhatsApp and email.
Workflow engine
LangGraph
Graph execution for all 13 steps. Human-in-the-loop interrupt nodes for AM gates. Checkpoint to Firestore for recovery.
Agent runtime
Nanobot (HKUDS)
5 specialised agents: Brief, Delivery, Feedback, Escalation, Intelligence. Stateless — context injected at invocation by LangGraph.
Storage (MVP)
Firebase (Firestore + Storage)
Firestore for all structured data and event log. Firebase Storage for files. Real-time listeners replace WebSocket complexity.
Auth
Firebase Auth
Google sign-in. Single whitelisted AM account for MVP. JWT with Firebase ID token on every API request.
Connectors
MCP protocol
WhatsApp MCP, Microsoft Graph (Outlook), Gemini audio, Flask.do (TBC). Each is a discrete MCP server — swappable without agent changes.
LLM
Claude Sonnet
Primary reasoning model for all 5 agents. Gemini audio-native for call transcription (tone and prosody preservation).
Storage (v2)
PostgreSQL + Redis
Schema-per-tenant isolation, row-level security, LangGraph native PostgreSQL checkpointer. Redis for AM queue and WebSocket broadcast.
07 — Market context
What Relay is not competing with
Understanding where Relay sits clarifies what it is.
CRMs
HubSpot, Salesforce
CRMs store contact data. Relay executes workflows. A CRM is a database. Relay is an agent that acts.
Project management
Asana, Linear, Notion
These track tasks for internal teams. Relay manages the client relationship — the external-facing layer.
Agency platforms
Function Point, Teamwork
Generic agency tools built for project management, not for agentic client communication. No AI layer.
AI chat tools
Intercom AI, Tidio
These handle customer support chat. Relay handles complex multi-step project workflows with formal agreements, pricing, and sign-offs.
WhatsApp tools
AiSensy, WATI
These broadcast and respond via WhatsApp. Relay uses WhatsApp as one channel in a broader workflow — it reasons, it gates, it acts.
Nearest analogue
Nothing quite like this exists
Relay is what happens when you take the full client-facing AM workflow and build an agentic layer underneath it. This category doesn't have a name yet.
08 — Build sequence
From working demo to live system
Each step is a working system at the end. Never start the next step until the current one is tested with real data. BlinkofAI goes live at step 8. Steps 9 onwards productise it.

Content work — requires Sandeep. Nothing downstream works without these.
Markdown files · Firebase Storage · manual authoring
02
Firestore schema + Firebase project
Create all collections: clients, projects, messages, am_queue, events, workflow_state. Create Firebase Storage buckets: call_recordings, agreements, assets, kb. Set up Firebase Auth with Google sign-in, whitelist the AM's Google account.
Firestore · Firebase Storage · Firebase Auth
03
Brief agent — isolated test
Build and test the Brief agent in Nanobot. Load KB files as skills from Firebase Storage. Test with 10 real historical briefs from BlinkofAI. Verify: project type classification, completeness scoring, gap question generation, structured brief output. Do not wire to LangGraph yet.
Nanobot · Claude Sonnet · brief_taxonomy skill
04
LangGraph graph — F01 + F02
Build the workflow graph with F01 (brief intake) and F02 (evaluation + pricing) as the first two nodes. F02 uses interrupt_before — AM gate. Test the full checkpoint-suspend-resume cycle manually. Verify: graph pauses at F02, AM decision injected, graph resumes, project_card written to Firestore.

05
FastAPI backend + WhatsApp MCP
Build FastAPI with Firebase Admin SDK auth. Wire WhatsApp MCP. Build inbound webhook receiver: message arrives → normalised → written to Firestore messages collection → LangGraph graph triggered for that project. Test the full path: WhatsApp brief → agent runs → am_queue item written.

06
Outlook connector
Wire Microsoft Graph API. Poll every 5 minutes. Match inbound emails to client by sender email address. Normalise to message schema. Write to messages collection. Test: client sends email → appears in project thread.

Build client list, project detail (overview + workflow tracker + message thread), AM queue panel, flags strip. Wire Firestore real-time listeners for live updates. Add Delivery (F03–F06), Feedback (F07–F09), Escalation (F10–F11), Intelligence (F12–F13) agents. Add all remaining graph nodes. Wire Gemini audio for call transcription.
